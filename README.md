Stock Trading Platform
### ⚙️ Backend Features

* **JWT Authentication & Authorization** with Spring Security.
* **Role-Based Access Control** (`ROLE_ADMIN`, `ROLE_USER`).
* **User Management** — registration, login, CRUD operations.
* **Product Management** — create, update, delete, and retrieve products.
* **Search, Filtering & Sorting** for products.
* **Favorite/Wishlist Management** — add and remove favorite products.
* **Shopping Basket Management** — add, remove, update quantity, and clear basket.
* **Payment Processing** — balance validation, payment, and stock updates.
* **Wallet & Deposit System** — manage user balance and deposits.
* **Automatic Basket Expiration** — basket items are automatically removed after **24 hours**.
* **PostgreSQL Database** integration with Spring Data JPA/Hibernate.
* **RESTful APIs** for frontend-backend communication.
* **Exception Handling** for invalid requests and business errors.
* **DTO-based Architecture** for secure and structured data transfer.
